## 10. Monitor app: draft spec (thresholds are provisional, from one run)

| Check | Rule | Catches |
|---|---|---|
| Pump not running | Switch on and power below 2 W after 10 s (or `accessory` still off) | Pump unplugged or dead, broken wire |
| Pump stuck on | Switch off and power above 2 W after 15 s | Stuck relay, something else controlling the plug |
| Running light | Steady-state average (60 s to end) below 6.2 W | Worn or stretched peristaltic hose, slipping rollers, running dry |
| Running heavy | Steady-state average above 7.3 W | Clogged injection point, kinked or crystallized line, cold stiff hose |
| Run length | Actual on time differs from planned by more than 5 s | Dropped commands, missed stop |
| Slow drift | 7-run rolling average moves more than 5% from baseline | Gradual hose wear well before failure |

Design notes: once 7 to 10 runs on 2.30 exist, replace the fixed limits with baseline mean plus or minus 3 standard deviations of the per-run steady-state averages. Use watts, not amps (0.1 W versus about 1.2 W resolution). A short motor inrush cannot be seen by a Z-Wave plug. Evening temperature likely affects hose stiffness, so consider logging ambient temperature per run. The profiler's `state.history` and the summary CSV are the natural data source; the monitor could be merged into the profiler app or read its summary file. Notify through PushOver (device 1955), matching app 2200.

## 11. Tool and platform gotchas learned

- **Swap Device refuses component child devices** (ZEN14 and ZEN20 outlets). Edit each app's setting instead, or keep IDs and repoint network IDs.
- **Component child network IDs can only be changed by the parent's driver** (`childDevice.setDeviceNetworkId()`); `hub_update_device` refuses them.
- **Built-in "Zooz Power Strip" maps outlet reports to children by network ID** (`<parentDNI>-CH<n>`), so a re-included strip needs its children renamed.
- **Rule Machine:** raw `settings` writes to action fields are dropped on mainPage; use `replaceActions` with `{capability:'switch', action:'on', deviceIds:[id]}` and `{capability:'delay', minutes:1}`.
- **`hub_set_native_app` button clicks also submit Done**, which fires `updated()`.
- **The jtp driver caches `firmwareVersion`** and only re-reads it when empty; `updateDataValue` cannot clear it via the MCP tool. Check firmware in Settings > Z-Wave Details.
- `hub_get_device_attribute` requires `expectedValue`; `hub_list_devices` `attributeNames` only works with `format='context'`.
- A Z-Wave SmartStart "Device added successfully" message can appear even when nothing joined; trust the node table, not the dialog.
- The hub radio can get stuck "Busy" on a phantom node; a full shutdown and 60 s power removal clears it (a soft reboot does not reset the radio).
